# RIVALS CS2 Season 2: LEGENDS

Four places at LAN. SEK 30,000 on the line. RIVALS CS2 LEGENDS gives teams a chance to compete, build a name and earn their place in the finals through open online qualification.

The season is scheduled for **November 3, 2026, to January 30, 2027**, with the title decided at the RIVALS studio at GRO36 in Jönköping. Finalist teams must arrive on January 29 for media obligations. Entry is free. Every match is best of three (BO3), and every place at LAN must be earned.

## Tournament format

### Qualifier Stage: 16 teams

Teams are split into **two groups of eight**. Each group plays a single round robin, with every team facing each other team in its group once.

- All matches are BO3.
- The schedule is planned around an average of one match per team per week.
- All group matches must finish by January 10, 2027.
- The top two teams in each group qualify directly for LAN.
- No quarterfinals are played in this format.

At LAN, the winner of Group A faces the runner-up from Group B. The winner of Group B faces the runner-up from Group A.

### Qualifier Stage: 17 to 32 teams

Teams are split into **four groups of up to eight**. Each group plays a single round robin, with every team facing each other team in its group once.

- All matches are BO3.
- The schedule is planned around an average of one match per team per week.
- All group matches must finish by January 10, 2027.
- The top two teams in each group advance to the online quarterfinals.
- Each of those eight teams plays one BO3 for a place at LAN.
- Two quarterfinals are played on January 16, 2027, and two on January 17, 2027.
- The four quarterfinal winners qualify for LAN.

The quarterfinals will be scheduled so fans can follow all four matches on Twitch.

The playoff bracket is fixed and published before the group stage begins. Quarterfinal places are determined by final group standings. Winners advance along the published bracket without reseeding, including when moving from the online quarterfinals to the LAN semifinals. An approved replacement inherits the withdrawing team's bracket position and remaining path.

## Seeding and advancement

Teams are initially ordered by their official Global VRS rank from the specified invitation publication. Teams without a Global VRS rank are seeded below all VRS-ranked teams. Their order is determined by HLTV World Ranking points, highest first, followed by random seeding for remaining ties. The HLTV ranking is separate from VRS. Group results then determine advancement under the published format.

| Detail | Method |
| --- | --- |
| VRS publication used for initial seeding | Official Global VRS invitation publication dated October 5, 2026. |
| Ordering unranked teams and resolving seed ties | 1. HLTV World Ranking points, highest first. 2. Random seeding for remaining ties. These criteria do not override distinct ranks in the official VRS order. |
| HLTV ranking snapshot | The latest HLTV World Ranking available on October 22, 2026. Treatment of teams without HLTV ranking points is pending confirmation. |
| Group allocation | Snake seeding using the final seed order: A, B, B, A for two groups, or A, B, C, D, D, C, B, A for four groups. The sequence repeats until all teams are placed; group sizes differ by at most one. |
| Group standings | Three points for a series win and zero points for a series loss. |
| Group tiebreakers | Applied in order: 1. Head-to-head results between the tied teams. 2. Round differential in matches between the tied teams. 3. Overall group round differential. |
| Forfeits | A forfeit counts as a regular series win or loss, awarding three points to the winner and zero to the loser. A full BO3 forfeit is recorded as a 2-0 series win. Each of the two awarded maps contributes +1 round differential to the winner and -1 to the loser, for a total of +2 and -2 respectively. |
| Advancement with 16 teams | Top two per group to LAN; A1 vs B2 and B1 vs A2 |
| Advancement with 17 to 32 teams | Top two per group to the fixed quarterfinal bracket above; winners advance to LAN without reseeding. QF1 and QF2 feed semifinal 1; QF3 and QF4 feed semifinal 2. |
